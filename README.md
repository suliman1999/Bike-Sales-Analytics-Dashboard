## 📊 Overview
This project presents an interactive **Power BI Sales Analytics Dashboard** focused on analyzing bike retail revenue across multiple cities and stores.  
It provides insights into **total revenue, sales trends, brand performance, category distribution**, and **geographic sales patterns**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze total revenue and sales trends over time
- Compare revenue performance by store and brand
- Understand revenue distribution across cities and categories
- Identify top-performing brands and stores
- Support data-driven retail decision-making

---

## 🚀 Key Features
- Total revenue KPI with dynamic filters
- Revenue analysis by store name
- Revenue breakdown by brand
- Geographic revenue distribution by city (map)
- Revenue analysis by city and product category
- Revenue trends by year
- Interactive slicers for city and date range
